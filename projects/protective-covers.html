Project Overview

I designed three reusable TPU protective covers for the Boeing 767 assembly line. The work connected hazard identification, CATIA modeling, FDM prototyping, fit checks, and integration into the documented production process.

Problem Statement

Certain areas of the 767 assembly process involved exposed rotating motors and other components that presented EHS hazards to production workers. Existing solutions were inadequate or non-existent, and parts in these areas were also susceptible to scratching and damage during assembly operations. A reusable, low-cost protective solution was needed that could be formally integrated into the production workflow.

My Contribution

  • Identified EHS hazard areas and part damage risks on the active 767 production floor
  • Designed 3 custom protective covers in CATIA tailored to specific components including rotating motor housings and other hazardous areas
  • Selected NinjaFlex TPU as the material for its flexibility, durability, and reusability
  • Produced covers via FDM 3D printing and validated fit and protection on the production floor
  • Integrated the covers into the official Boeing 767 production plan as documented assembly-process aids

Outcome

All three protective covers were formally added to the Boeing 767 production plan and implemented on the active assembly line. The reusable TPU design addressed identified exposure and part-damage risks through documented assembly-process aids.

Tools Used

  • CATIA — 3D CAD design
  • FDM 3D Printing with NinjaFlex TPU — flexible, reusable part production
