767 assembly line protective covers

Reusable TPU covers designed for hazardous areas on an active aircraft assembly line.

Inside this projectProject OverviewProblem StatementMy ContributionOutcomeTools Used

Project Overview

During my internship at Boeing, I identified safety and EHS-related risks on the 767 production floor where exposed rotating motors and other hazardous areas posed potential risks to operators and builders during assembly. I designed, prototyped, and implemented a series of reusable protective covers to mitigate these hazards, which were subsequently added to the official 767 production plan.

Problem Statement

Certain areas of the 767 assembly process involved exposed rotating motors and other components that presented EHS hazards to production workers. Existing solutions were inadequate or non-existent, and parts in these areas were also susceptible to scratching and damage during assembly operations. A reusable, low-cost protective solution was needed that could be formally integrated into the production workflow.

My Contribution

  • Identified EHS hazard areas and part damage risks on the active 767 production floor
  • Designed 3 custom protective covers in CATIA tailored to specific components including rotating motor housings and other hazardous areas
  • Selected NinjaFlex TPU as the material for its flexibility, durability, and reusability
  • Produced covers via FDM 3D printing and validated fit and protection on the production floor
  • Personally implemented the covers into the official Boeing 767 production plan, making them a formally documented part of the assembly process

Outcome

All three protective covers were formally added to the Boeing 767 production plan and implemented on the active assembly line. The reusable TPU design reduced part damage risk and improved EHS compliance for operators and builders, with a solution that will continue to be used beyond the internship.

Tools Used

  • CATIA — 3D CAD design
  • FDM 3D Printing with NinjaFlex TPU — flexible, reusable part production
