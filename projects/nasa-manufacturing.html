Research Context

At NASA Marshall Space Flight Center, my internship focused on sensing and software systems for laser directed energy deposition (LDED).

Real-Time Monitoring

I integrated multi-camera sensing and real-time inference on an NVIDIA Jetson Thor. A decoupled Holoscan pipeline coordinated subsystems operating at different rates, with results communicated through a physical LED display.

Engineering Decisions

  • Separate processing rates: Structure the sensing and analytics pipeline around decoupled subsystems rather than one blocking sequence.
  • Connect software to hardware: Bring camera inference and analytics into a physical feedback interface.
Mechanical Engineering Meets Embedded Systems

This work extends my manufacturing background into process monitoring and automation: understanding the physical operation, integrating sensing, coordinating computation, and communicating useful information.

Tools & Methods

  • NVIDIA Jetson Thor and GPU-accelerated inference
  • Holoscan and multi-rate pipeline design
  • Multi-camera sensing and real-time detection

What I Learned

I learned to design around the different rates at which sensors, inference, and physical feedback operate. Decoupling those tasks makes the system architecture as important as the individual algorithm. Connecting the analysis to a physical feedback interface kept the work tied to the manufacturing operation.
