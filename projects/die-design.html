Overview

Developed injection molding dies for aerospace investment-casting tooling applications, including gates, soluble cores, and soluble chills. I built a reusable parametric SolidWorks template and prepared approximately four complete die designs, focusing on repeatable layout, mold separation, and machinable geometry.

What I Did

  • Parameterization: Linked a SolidWorks die template to an external text file of tool-stock dimensions so updates propagated through the model. Included airboxes, lifting holes, locating pins, keeper screws, and dowel-rod locations.
  • Tool Layout: Sized each tool and positioned its components relative to the part geometry.
  • Mold Separation: Matched split lines across die pieces to the part split line.
  • Machinability: Resolved undercuts and backlocks through contoured split surfaces suitable for 3-axis CNC milling, or removable inserts where geometry required them.
  • Flow Assessment: Used FEA wax-flow analysis on selected designs to assess modeled filling across the tool cavity.

Deliverables at Project Handoff

  • Completed approximately four full die designs, with CNC-ready models prepared for manufacturing workflows.
  • Delivered a reusable parametric template so subsequent die layouts share common stock dimensions and assembly features.
  • Customer approval and full production status are separate from the completed design deliverables.

Tools & Methods

  • CAD: SolidWorks (parametric modeling, surfacing, split line design)
  • Analysis: FEA wax flow simulation
  • Manufacturing Consideration: 3-axis CNC milling, insert design
