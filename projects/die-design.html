Designing casting tooling and a reusable parametric die template.

Overview

Designed injection molding dies from scratch for a range of investment casting tooling applications in aerospace production, including gates, soluble cores, and soluble chills. Developed a reusable parametric die template in SolidWorks that has streamlined the tooling design process across multiple projects.

What I Did

  • Designed a parametric SolidWorks injection molding die template linked to an external text file containing tool stock dimensions, allowing key parameters to be updated and automatically reflected throughout the model. The template includes all standard die features including airboxes, lifting holes, locating pins, keeper screws, and dowel rod locations for die assembly
  • Used the template as a foundation for each new tool, configuring sizing and layout by positioning and spacing all die components relative to the part geometry
  • Matched split lines across all die pieces to the part split line, ensuring proper mold separation
  • Identified and resolved undercuts and backlocks by designing custom split surfaces, either as contoured geometry millable on a 3-axis CNC machine or as removable inserts to allow complex surface features to be machined and assembled
  • Occasionally ran FEA wax flow analysis to verify wax fill across the full tool cavity

Deliverables at Project Handoff

  • Completed approximately 4 full die designs, with CNC-ready models prepared for manufacturing workflows
  • The parametric template created for this work is now a reusable standard that reduces setup time for future die designs
  • Customer approval and full production status are separate from the completed design deliverables

Tools & Methods

  • CAD: SolidWorks (parametric modeling, surfacing, split line design)
  • Analysis: FEA wax flow simulation
  • Manufacturing Consideration: 3-axis CNC milling, insert design

Note: Specific part numbers, customer names, and proprietary design details are omitted in accordance with ITAR regulations and confidentiality agreements.
