Gating system optimization

Reverse engineering legacy gates and improving aerospace investment casting designs.

Inside this projectOverviewWhat I DidImpactTools & Methods

Overview

Designed and optimized gating systems for investment cast aerospace components, combining reverse engineering of legacy gating with FEA solidification modeling to improve casting quality and design for manufacturability.

What I Did

  • Reverse engineered existing gating systems that lacked CAD models, including cases where legacy epoxy tooling required bluelight scanning to capture geometry and reconstruct accurate 3D models
  • Attached reconstructed or newly designed gate geometry to CAD pattern models and ran FEA solidification simulations to identify hotspots and predict defect locations
  • Redesigned gating configurations to improve solidification behavior and reduce predicted defects, while also optimizing split lines and overall geometry for better tooling and manufacturability
  • Applied design for manufacturing and assembly principles throughout, ensuring gating designs are practical to tool and produce at scale

Impact

  • Completed 3 full gating system designs; 2 of 3 have been validated through solidification modeling with favorable results
  • 1 design currently in revision based on simulation feedback
  • Gate packages pending customer approval before entering production

Tools & Methods

  • CAD: SolidWorks
  • Metrology: Bluelight Scanning
  • Analysis: FEA Solidification Simulation
  • Methodology: Reverse Engineering, Design for Manufacturing (DFM), Design for Assembly (DFA)

Note: Specific part numbers, customer names, and proprietary design details are omitted in accordance with ITAR regulations and confidentiality agreements.
