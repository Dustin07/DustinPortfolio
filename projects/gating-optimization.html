Gating System Optimization

Reverse engineering legacy gates and improving aerospace investment casting designs.

My Role

Reconstruct gate CAD and iterate geometry using solidification modeling.

Key Decision

Balance predicted solidification behavior with tooling split lines and manufacturability.

Outcome

3 gate packages; 2 showed favorable simulation results at handoff.

Evidence

Simulation-reviewed designs

Inside This ProjectOverviewWhat I DidDeliverables at Project HandoffTools & Methods

Overview

Reconstructed legacy gating geometry and developed three gate packages for investment-cast aerospace components. I used solidification simulations to guide design iterations while accounting for tooling split lines and manufacturability. Two designs showed favorable modeled results; customer approval was pending at handoff.

What I Did

  • Reverse engineered existing gating systems that lacked CAD models, including cases where legacy epoxy tooling required blue-light scanning to capture geometry and reconstruct accurate 3D models
  • Attached reconstructed or newly designed gate geometry to CAD pattern models and ran FEA solidification simulations to identify hotspots and predict defect locations
  • Redesigned gating configurations to improve solidification behavior and reduce predicted defects, while also optimizing split lines and overall geometry for better tooling and manufacturability
  • Applied design for manufacturing and assembly principles throughout, ensuring gating designs are practical to tool and produce at scale

Deliverables at Project Handoff

  • Completed 3 full gating system designs; 2 of 3 showed favorable solidification-modeling results at handoff
  • 1 design remained in revision based on simulation feedback at handoff
  • Gate packages were awaiting customer approval at handoff

Tools & Methods

  • CAD: SolidWorks
  • Metrology: Blue-Light Scanning
  • Analysis: FEA Solidification Simulation
  • Methodology: Reverse Engineering, Design for Manufacturing (DFM), Design for Assembly (DFA)
