Legacy Component Reverse Engineering

Reconciling legacy part models and drawings to support continued production.

Inside this projectOverviewWhat I DidImpactTools & Methods

Overview

Led model-to-drawing review and reverse engineering efforts for legacy investment cast aerospace components, many of which have been in continuous production for decades without modern CAD models. This work directly enables the procurement and manufacture of new injection molding tooling for parts at the end of their die lifecycle.

What I Did

  • Reviewed customer-supplied CAD models against legacy engineering drawings, identifying discrepancies between the nominal model geometry and the drawn definition
  • Engaged in extended technical discussions with aerospace OEM customers spanning 1 to 3 weeks per part, resolving model-to-drawing conflicts and collaborating on drawing updates to improve dimensional completeness and measurability
  • For cases where customers lacked the capability to recreate models, independently reconstructed full SolidWorks models from legacy drawings, leveraging in-house bluelight scan data and CMM measurements of currently produced parts to capture true as-built geometry
  • Applied scan data processed in Polyworks and Geomagic Design X to define complex non-standard features such as underdimensioned flowpaths, lofts, and guide curves that could not be fully resolved from the drawing alone
  • Worked directly with customers to update and fully define drawings, ensuring all features are measurable and unambiguous for future tooling and inspection use

Impact

  • Completed approximately 10 model-to-drawing reviews, with each effort unlocking the ability to execute open purchase orders for new or refurbished injection molding tooling
  • Several reviewed and approved parts have since moved into active tooling design and production
  • Enabled aerospace engine OEM customers to modernize aging production programs without disrupting ongoing manufacturing

Tools & Methods

  • CAD: SolidWorks
  • Metrology & Scan Processing: Bluelight Scanning, CMM, Polyworks, Geomagic Design X
  • Process: Model-to-drawing reconciliation, geometric dimensioning, customer technical review

Note: Specific part numbers, customer names, and proprietary design details are omitted in accordance with ITAR regulations and confidentiality agreements.
